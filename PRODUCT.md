# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Persian-speaking friend groups (typically 8 to 14 players) playing the social-deduction game Mafia in person: living rooms, cafés, game nights, usually late in the evening with the lights low. Everyone holds a phone. Players use the app to join a lobby, privately see their secret role, follow the day/night flow and review results. One person is the moderator (گرداننده), who runs the whole session from a phone: building the lobby, dealing roles, recording night actions, eliminating players and publishing the game report. A small admin group manages roles, scenarios, users and backups.

## Product Purpose
نقاب (Neghab, "mask") is the game board for in-person Mafia, part of Turku Games (turkugames.com). It replaces paper role cards and the moderator's notebook: dealing secret roles, tracking who is alive, logging nights, and turning each game into a shareable story afterwards. Success: a moderator can run a full game one-handed without breaking the table's atmosphere, and players feel the drama (the role reveal, the night falling, the final verdict) on their own phones.

## Brand Personality
Cinematic, sly, social. The tension of a crime drama (Peaky Blinders, Narcos title cards) delivered with the confidence and polish of a modern social app (Discord, Spotify), and the playful readability of social-deduction games (Among Us, Werewolf apps). Persian at its core: language, type and cultural motifs are native, never an afterthought or a translation layer.

## Anti-references
- The current "command center" UI: graphite + lime + blue, tech grid, dashboard chips. Reads as an admin tool, not a game.
- Generic SaaS dashboards: stat-chip rows, identical card grids, sidebar-plus-table layouts on player screens.
- Cliché mafia kitsch: fedoras, tommy guns, cigar smoke, black-red-gold casino palettes.
- Crowded instrument-panel UIs with many simultaneous panels (rejected before on other projects as "crowded and confusing").
- Flat "AI-generated" looks: white-on-white surfaces, icon-in-rounded-square everywhere.

## Design Principles
1. **The table comes first.** Everything is glanceable from a phone held at arm's length in a dark room, operable with one thumb, and never so bright it lights up the table or leaks a secret.
2. **Secrets feel secret.** Role reveals, night actions and alignments are staged moments with deliberate ceremony; nothing private is ever visible by accident.
3. **One bold surface per screen.** Identity comes from a confident color field, typography and one signature element, not from more panels.
4. **Drama at the moments, calm in between.** Phase changes, eliminations and the final verdict get theatrical treatment; routine controls stay quiet and familiar.
5. **Persian-native.** RTL, Persian type and Persian visual culture are the foundation of the identity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Faction colors are always paired with a shape or label, never color alone. Touch targets at least 44px. Full support for prefers-reduced-motion (staged reveals fall back to crossfades). Dark-first for low-light play, with a light day mode of equal legibility.
